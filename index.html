How to Solve a Rubik's Cube (Beginner's Version)


White Cross

To solve white cross, create a daisy with white edge pieces and the yellow center piece. After, look at the second colour (Usually Red, Orange, Blue, and Green) match it with the corrisponding center, and bring it down. Repeat this step until you get a white cross. It's also important to remember that the Red center is always opposite to the Orange center, Blue is always opposite of Green, and White is always opposite to Yellow.


First Layer

After the cross is solved, flip the cross so it's at the bottom of the cube for the rest of the solve. Then,find the corner pieces with white on them and look at the two other colours on that corner piece. After that, sandwich the piece in between those two other colors and use your right hand and bring the right side up, turn the upper face left with your index finger, turn the right side down with your wrist, then turn the upper face to the right with your left index finger. After doing this repeating this step 4 times, first layer should be done and look like this.


Second Layer

To solve the second layer of a rubik's cube you first find an edge (pieces that only have two colors) on the top layer that does not have yellow anywhere.Then, line up the color of the edge that is facing towards you to the correct center. After that, look at the top color of the edge. Depending on what the color is, you turn the top layer away from the center that matches the top color. For example, if the side color or the color facing you is blue, you turn the top layer to the blue center to line it up. If the top color is red, the two centers you can turn it to are always going to either have the top edge's color or the opposite colour of that color, in this case, red and orange, but you should to turn it to orange since it's away from red. After turning that edge away, if you turned it to the left, do a righty and then a white corner will face towards you so rotate the cube so the corner will face away from you while the white is still on the side and do a lefty which is using your left wrist to turn the left side up, using your left index finger to push the top layer to the right, using your left wrist to turn the left face down, then use your right index finger to turn the top layer to the left. If you turned it to the right, instead do a lefty then face the slot with the white from the corner to the right and do a righty. If there edges with only yellow on the top layer and second layer isn't solved yet, find the edge that isn't solved and rotate the cube so the slot that isn't solved is at the bottom right and do a righty, turn the cube so the white faces to the side and do a lefty.


Cubic Notation

Before learning last layer, I highly recommend learning cubic notation. An example is righty is R U R' U'

Last Layer

First step to solving last layer is to make a yellow cross. There are 3 cases for a cross, dot case, hook case and line case. For dot case, do F (Righty) F'. This should bring you to the next case, the hook. It's two yellow edges facing up creating a hook shape. To get a cross, position the hook so the edges are positioned to the right and down and do f (R U R' U') f '. This should create a yellow cross. For line cases, two yellow edges are facing up creating a yellow line. Position the line so that it's horizontal and do F (R U R' U') F' to create a cross. After this, you need to move the cross edges so that they match the correct center. To do this, check if two edges need to do an ajacent swap, move the solved edges to the back and right of the cube and do an algorithm called a Sune (R U R' U R U2 R') and line up the cross. If the edges that need to swap are opposite of each other, put the solved edges to the left and right of the cube and do a Sune. After that, you should get the case discussed previously. Next, you should have 2 to 4 corners remaining. We need to permutate those corners to the correct position. There can either be none permutated corners, one permutated corners, or all permutated corners. If you have one permutated corner, postition that corner so it's at the bottom left and do an algorithm called Niklas (R U' L' U R' U' L). Sometimes you need to do this more than once and if you have no corners permutated, then do Niklas from any position to permutate one corner. When all corner are permutated, flip the cube so white faces to the top and when you reach a corner that isn't twisted correctly, do righties until that corner looks twisted correctly. It might look like the cube is getting messed up, but thats all part of the plan. When you finish twisting the corner correctly, DO NOT ROTATE THE CUBE! Use the D layer to align to another incorrectly twisted corner and repeat this step. After this, congrats! You solved your first cube! Keep practicing to get better and better! Remember, all pro cuber were once beginners like you!
