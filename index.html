How to Solve a Rubik's Cube (Beginner's Version)


White Cross

To solve white cross, create a daisy with white edge pieces and the yellow center piece. After, look at the second colour (Usually Red, Orange, Blue, and Green) match it with the corrisponding center, and bring it down. Repeat this step until you get a white cross.


First Layer

After the cross is solved, flip the cross so it's at the bottom of the cube for the rest of the solve. Then,find the corner pieces with white on them and look at the two other colours on that corner piece. After that, sandwich the piece in between those two other colors and use your right hand and bring the right side up, turn the upper face left with your index finger, turn the right side down with your wrist, then turn the upper face to the right with your left index finger.
