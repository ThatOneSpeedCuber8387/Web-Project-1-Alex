How to Solve a Rubik's Cube (CFOP Method)

Cross

Similar to the Beginner's method, the first step is to solve a white cross. The difference is that you will solve the cross directly without creating a daisy. This step mainly includes positioning white edges into their correct place and aligning the cross to the correct centers. A white cross can always be solved in 8 moves or less.

First Two Layers (F2L)

F2L is the step after solving the cross. This step involves solving the first two layers at the same time. It starts off by bringing two matching edge and corner pieces, solving them into an F2L pair, and inserting the pair into the correct slots. With beginner F2L, there is an extra step of seperating the edge and corner pieces if they are connected together incorrectly.
