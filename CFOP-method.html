How to Solve a Rubik's Cube (CFOP Method)

Cross

Similar to the Beginner's method, the first step is to solve a white cross. The difference is that you will solve the cross directly without creating a daisy. This step mainly includes positioning white edges into their correct place and aligning the cross to the correct centers. A white cross can always be solved in 8 moves or less.

First Two Layers (F2L)

F2L is the step after solving the cross. This step involves solving the first two layers at the same time. It starts off by bringing two matching edge and corner pieces, solving them into an F2L pair, and inserting the pair into the correct slots. With beginner F2L, there is an extra step of seperating the edge and corner pieces if they are connected together incorrectly.

OLL

Orientation of the Last Layer (OLL) is the second to last step in the CFOP method. This step orients the top face on last layer so they are all the same color. Normally, there are 57 different OLL cases, but with 2-look OLL, there are only 7 different cases to memorize (Found in OLL section or in video below). The first step of 2-look, is to orient the edges on the top face or in other words, to make a yellow cross similar to the beginner's method. Then, you do 1 of 7 different algorithms one of them being Sune, to orient the yellow sticker to the top face.


PLL

Permutation of the Last Layer (PLL) is the last step in the CFOP method. This step permutes all remaining pieces on the last layer to their correct position. There are 21 different PLL cases, but with 2-look PLL, there are only 5 different cases to memorize. The first step of 2-look PLL is to permute the corners. There are 2 different cases to permute the corners. You can tell what case you have by checking the corners to see if your case has headlights which is when two corners show the same color on the same side. If your case has headlights, (Adjacent corner swap) you position the headlights to the left of the cube and do an algorithm called T-Perm (Algorithms in PLL section or in video below). But, if your case doesn't have headlights, (Diagonal corner swap) then you do an algorithm called Y-Perm from any angle to solve the corners. After solving the corners, you now permute the edges. There are 4 different cases, Ua and Ub perm, H perm, and Z perm (Edge-only cases). After solving the edges, the cube will be solved.
